# 1934 World Cup

January 1934. Host: **E.S.U.** The hosting rights were Calveria's, earned through the 1933 Nations League, and passed to the E.S.U. on Calveria's annexation. The E.S.U. therefore entered without playing a qualifier.

32 teams: 10 from EUFA, 8 from CONSEAF, 8 from PFA, 6 from VAFC.
