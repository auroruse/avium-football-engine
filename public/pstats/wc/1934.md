# 1934 World Cup

January 1934. Host: **E.S.U.** The hosting rights were Calveria's, earned through the 1933 Nations League, and passed to the E.S.U. on Calveria's annexation. The E.S.U. therefore entered without playing a qualifier.

32 teams: 10 from EUFA, 8 from CONSEAF, 8 from PFA, 6 from VAFC.

## Notes

### Notes

**Champions: E.S.U.**, unbeaten, as hosts, having entered without a qualifying campaign.

**Ponurvia lost their first match of the tournament in the final.** They came through Group F ahead of Skjarnland, then beat Polabiny, Hōrai and Reino.

**Nichirin finished fourth.** They had qualified with five wins from five and no goals conceded, and won the 1933 Nations League.

Alemannia, the defending champions, lost 0-1 to Mizuho in their opening match, qualified second in Group D on three points, and went out in the Round of 16.

Elvester won the EUFA qualifier with two goals conceded in five matches and lost all three group games.

Group stage leaders — Goals: S. Nakhtmin (KMT) 5. Assists: C. Pei (GUA) 2. Rating: S. Shakibaii (VAR) 8.4. Chances created: R. Prokhorov (RUD) 13, M. Kishima (NCH) 13. Defensive contributions: S. Mehrian (VAR) 9. Saves: N. Papadopoulos (AST) 19.

### Continuity flags

- Full-tournament player statistics are not recorded. The 1933 edition keeps these in a separate `Player Stats.tsv`; the figures above cover the group stage only.
- The World Cup is played in **Evrian summer**, which falls in January. The Senpan Tribune of 6 December 1933 refers to the tournament as "next summer" on that basis, six weeks out.

### Continuity flags

- **E.S.U. host, and so did not enter the CONSEAF qualifier.** The hosting rights were originally Calveria's, earned on the strength of their 1933 Nations League run, in which they knocked out Reino, Guandong and Karjania. Calveria subsequently reached an agreement with the E.S.U. to be annexed, and the tournament passed to the E.S.U. with everything else. Calveria no longer appear in `Programs/Avium Map/src/data/nations.tsv` or in the football preset `AVIUM.tsv`.
- **The CONSEAF field included an Independent XI**, a scratch side of unaffiliated players entered to fill the schedule and never eligible for a berth. It finished 7th; the last eligible place went to Auritania, 8th.
- The 1933 World Cup was hosted by Nichirin and won by Alemannia.
- **Host eligibility.** Hosting passes to the highest-placed *eligible* finisher of the preceding Nations League. Nichirin won the 1933 edition but had just hosted the 1933 World Cup. Reino finished second but are Evrian, and Alemannia's 1932 hosting bars Evrian nations until 1935. Calveria, third, were the highest-placed side clear of both bars.
