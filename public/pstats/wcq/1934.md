## Notes

**Kinshū missed out on goal difference.** Level with Kemet on seven points, one goal worse off at +4 against +5, after drawing 2-2 with Kemet in the final round in the fixture that decided the place. Kinshū had knocked Alemannia and Skjarnland out of the 1933 Nations League six weeks earlier.
