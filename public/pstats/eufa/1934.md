# 1934 EUFA Championship

16 teams in two groups of eight, single round robin of 7 rounds. Top 3 from each group advance to a double-elimination bracket; the two group winners take byes into the upper semi-finals.

**Nichirin's first EUFA competition**, entered after the transfer from VAFC, and their only one. See [[Senpan Tribune 1934-02-01]] for the move and [[Senpan Tribune 1934-03-14]] for the return.

## Notes

### Notes

**Champions: Nichirin**, on their first entry, unbeaten across ten matches: seven group wins, then Elvester and Reino twice. 33 scored, 11 conceded.

**Reino conceded two goals in seven group matches and six in two matches against Nichirin.** They won Group B, lost the upper final, came back through the lower bracket by beating Skjarnland 5-1, and met Nichirin again in the grand final.

**Skjarnland finished third having beaten Reino in the group phase**, 1-0 in Round 2, the only defeat Reino took all tournament until the grand final. Reino then eliminated them twice.

**Both lower-bracket first-round ties were rematches of the upper quarter-finals, and the same side won both.** Arverne lost to Elvester twice, Karjania to Skjarnland twice. Neither second-placed side won a knockout match.

Elvester had won the 1934 World Cup qualifier conceding two goals in five matches, then lost all three group games at the World Cup. They finished fourth here.

### Continuity flags

- **Venue.** Under the terms of the VAFC-to-EUFA transfer, Nichirin play every EUFA fixture away, with nominal home games staged at the opponent's ground ([[Senpan Tribune 1934-02-01]]). The fixture list records no venues, so the arrangement is not visible in the results above.
- **Bracket seeding.** The upper semi-finals paired each group winner with a qualifier from their own group. The 1934 PFA Championship bracket the same season crossed them, pairing each group winner with a qualifier from the other group. The two competitions used the same stated format.
- **Field.** 16 teams, down from 17 in 1933. Genosa, Slytlia and Lechia did not enter; Nichirin and Turulia did.
