# 1933 VAFC Championship

15 teams, single league phase of 5 rounds. Top 8 advance to quarter-finals. 2-leg knockout ties.

Fifteen teams over five rounds leaves one bye per round; byes are recorded as 3-0 wins, so the table carries five more wins than losses and fifteen more goals for than against.
