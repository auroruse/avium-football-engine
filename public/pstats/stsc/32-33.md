## Notes

**Kyōwa Naginomiya** win their second cup and their first trophy since 93/94, when they lifted the same competition as Naginomiya Chishimahito. Thirty-nine years and four name changes between them.

**Ayanami Sei-Hachiman** lose the final as holders, having won it the previous season. They came through the quarter-final and semi-final as the underdog in both.

**Club Valtheria** reached the semi-finals, beating Toyama Thunderbolts on penalties, Kōshū Sentinels, and Spartak Kanagawa in the quarter-final before losing 3-0 to Naginomiya. They were relegated from NL1 the same season, finishing 19th.

**Shimonosaki Marina** knocked out Dynamo Mizuhara 2-0 in the Round of 16 without conceding.

**Spartak Kanagawa, Tsukumo Shinkeisei, Teito Shinkeisei, Kannushi and 1. FK Sen'ninhara** were all eliminated before the semi-finals, leaving the Shogun's own cup without a capital club in the last four.
