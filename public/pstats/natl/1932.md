## Notable Runs

**Varahmehr** — Dropped to losers bracket in WR64 (lost 1-2 to Côte de Saumon). Won 8 consecutive losers bracket matches including upsets over Karjania (82), Alemannia (83) on pens, E.S.U. (85) on pens, and Ponurvia (78) before falling to Seignid in the LB Semi-Final.

**Tonkin** — Upset Alemannia (83) in WR64, Hollosend (84) in WR16, and E.S.U. (85) on pens in the WB QF. Reached the WB Semi-Finals as a 72-rated side.

**Cahaya** — Beat Elvester (84) twice: 2-1 in WR64 and 4-2 in LB R4. Eliminated in LB R6 by Kinshū.

**Seignid** — 69-rated side reached the LB Final through seven straight wins including upsets over Auritania (81) on pens and Kinshū (75). Lost to Nichirin 1-2 in the LB Final.
